# AP Business 1.6 — Business Ethics Practice

An interactive, self-graded practice worksheet for **Unit 1.6 Business Ethics**
(AP Business with Personal Finance).

**Live page:** https://qwq-1025.github.io/business-ethics-practice/

## What it is

A single self-contained HTML file. No build step, no dependencies, no backend,
no network requests, no analytics, no cookies.

| Section | Items | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Essential Knowledge — Fill in the Blanks | 15 | 15 |
| 2 · Multiple Choice | 5 | 5 |
| 3 · Red Flag Detector (Northline Athletics) | 6 | 6 |
| 4 · Stakeholder Scorecard (Internal / External) | 6 | 6 |
| 5 · FRQ answers + finisher prompts (keyword-graded) | 9 fields | 23 |
| **Total** | | **55** |

The banner at the top tracks your running score as you check each section.

## How to use it

1. Open the link (works on phone and desktop).
2. Fill in your name, date and period if you want your progress saved.
3. Work through the sections and press the **Check** button on each one.
4. Press **💾 Save All Responses** when you are done.

Progress is saved in **your own browser only** (`localStorage`) — nothing is ever
uploaded anywhere. Clearing your browser data clears your answers.

## Editing

`index.html` is the whole thing — HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file. Edit it
and reload; there is nothing to build.
